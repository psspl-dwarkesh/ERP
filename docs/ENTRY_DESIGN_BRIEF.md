# Entry surface direction

Mode: Persuade. Audience: ABC Limited stakeholders and staff reviewing the ERP concept. Job: understand how one engineering job crosses modules, then enter the working demo. All record names and values are illustrative.

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** The short entry behaves like an engineering job traveller: a concrete record passes through handoffs. The page earns attention with a working chain, not a set of feature cards.

**OWN WORLD:** Warm drawing paper, ink-black and oxidised copper; condensed industrial lettering for headlines, legible sans for work text, tabular figures for IDs. Ruled sections, registration marks and one continuous route line establish the visual system. The app shell inherits this palette without turning operating screens into decoration.

**STORY:** A visitor sees one job, follows its order → project → materials → assembly → service chain, and enters the relevant module or dashboard.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** A large typographic declaration dominates the left and upper field. The lower and right field is a live, connected job route with an active record and next action. One high-contrast Enter workspace action stays visible in the top right and again beside the active record. No floating cards, soft abstract gradients or promotional chart preview.

**FORM:** Engineering job traveller / route sheet, grounded candidate four. Seed reference `seed:06e654a30d8bb5d9`. Signature interaction: selecting a handoff changes the highlighted route, its record evidence and the module entry action, and the change is legible as a record being restated: the active ID re-stamps its letter-spacing while a ruled scale plate in the summary column tracks the same stop. Decoration is drafting vocabulary only: a registration cross, rules and the route line.

**FINISH:** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
